About Me

I am Vibhoo Nikhil Sahu, a second-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Madhav Institute of Technology and Science, Gwalior. I am a passionate game developer with a strong focus on building immersive and technically efficient gameplay systems using Unity.

My core skill set includes Unity development, C# and C++, along with experience in Shader Graph, Blender (3D modeling, rigging, and basic animation), and Photoshop for UI design. I enjoy working across both the technical and creative sides of game development.

I have developed multiple projects, including a Taxi Simulator, Alien Runner: Planet Dash 3D, and I am currently working on a project called Well Worker. Among these, the Taxi Simulator stands out as my most refined and technically challenging project.

I particularly enjoy implementing unique and complex gameplay systems such as NPC spawn/despawn systems, crafting mechanics, and pathfinding using algorithms like Dijkstra. I am a fast learner and detail-oriented developer who enjoys solving problems and optimizing systems.

What excites me most about game development is the polishing phase—enhancing visuals and overall player experience using post-processing effects, Shader Graph, and fine-tuning gameplay details. My goal is to work in a AAA game studio and contribute to creating high-quality, engaging gaming experiences.
